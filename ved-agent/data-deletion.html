VED AGENT / DATA RIGHTS

Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: 9 October 2026

You can request deletion of personal information associated with your use of Ved Agent.

How to request deletion

  1. Email vedhuvai@protonmail.com with the subject “Ved Agent data deletion request”.
  2. Include the WhatsApp phone number you used to message the assistant, with its country code. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information.
  3. We may ask for reasonable confirmation that you control that number before processing the request.

What happens next

We will review the request and delete or de-identify information under the operator’s control where applicable, subject to legal and security requirements. We will aim to respond within 30 days. Copies that remain in platform backups or provider logs may be retained for a limited period under the respective providers’ retention policies. You may also need to contact Meta/WhatsApp or another provider directly for data held under its own account and retention controls.

Contact

For questions, email vedhuvai@protonmail.com.
