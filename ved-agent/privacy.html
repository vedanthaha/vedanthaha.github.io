VED AGENT / POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026

Ved Agent is an experimental WhatsApp assistant. This policy explains what information is processed when you message the assistant and how to request deletion.

Information processed

When you send a WhatsApp message to Ved Agent, the WhatsApp Business Platform may provide the sender’s phone-number identifier, available profile name, message text, message identifier, timestamp, and related delivery metadata. The workflow processes the message and sender identifier to generate and send a reply.

How information is used

  • To receive and respond to messages you send to Ved Agent.
  • To provide the message text to an AI model for response generation.
  • To diagnose failures, maintain the workflow, and protect the service from misuse.

Service providers

Ved Agent relies on Meta/WhatsApp to deliver messages, n8n Cloud to run the workflow, and Groq to generate AI responses. Information necessary for a response may be processed by these providers under their own terms, privacy notices, and retention practices. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identifiers, health information, or other sensitive information to this experimental assistant.

Retention and security

Message data is kept only as needed to operate, troubleshoot, and secure the service. Workflow execution records and provider logs may persist according to the retention settings and policies of the platforms involved. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Sharing and advertising

Ved Agent does not sell message content or phone-number information, and does not use it to build advertising profiles. Data is shared with the service providers described above as needed to deliver the service, or where required by law or to protect the service and its users.

Your choices

You can stop using the assistant by no longer messaging it. To request deletion of information under the operator’s control, follow the Data Deletion Instructions or email vedhuvai@protonmail.com.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the workflow or its data practices change. Questions about privacy can be sent to vedhuvai@protonmail.com.
